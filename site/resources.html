Check accreditation before you enrol. In Ethiopia no institution may start a programme before the Education and Training Authority (ETA, formerly HERQA) has accredited it. Look the programme up in ETA's accreditation register, or ask the institution for its accreditation certificate, before paying fees. More in the guide →
