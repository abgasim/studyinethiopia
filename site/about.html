About Study in Ethiopia

A free, independent guide that brings Ethiopia's higher-education information into one place.

Information about universities, colleges and scholarships in Ethiopia is spread across dozens of websites, many of them hard to find. Study in Ethiopia (SiE) collects it into one place so students, parents and professionals can compare options and go straight to the official source.

What you'll find here

  • Institutions: public and private universities and colleges, with short profiles and links to their own websites.
  • Study abroad: official study portals and scholarship bodies for Europe, the UK and the US.
  • English tests: where to take IELTS and TOEFL in Ethiopia, and free practice material.
  • Online learning: the main MOOC platforms for university-level courses you can take from home.

How we work

  • Official sources first. We link to institutions, ministries and government portals, not to agents.
  • Free to use. No sign-up, no fees.
  • Kept up to date. Institutions change names and websites often. If you spot something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

What's next

We're working on programme-level listings (what each institution teaches, in which language and mode), and on guides to the application process.

Is your institution missing or out of date? Send the correct name and website to the SiE team and we'll update the directory.
