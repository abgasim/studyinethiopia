About Study in Ethiopia

A free, independent guide that brings Ethiopia's higher-education information into one place.

Information about universities, colleges and scholarships in Ethiopia is spread across dozens of websites, many of them hard to find. Study in Ethiopia (SiE) collects it into one place so students, parents and professionals can compare options and go straight to the official source.

What you'll find here

  • Institutions: public and private universities and colleges, with profiles, a map, and links to their own websites.
  • Programmes: what each institution teaches, searchable by level, field and region, with a link to the page where each programme is listed.
  • Studying in Ethiopia from abroad: who regulates higher education and the steps to apply, get certificates recognised and get a student visa.
  • Study abroad: official study portals and scholarship bodies for Europe, the UK and the US, plus IELTS and TOEFL.
  • Online learning: the main platforms for university-level courses you can take from home.

How we work

  • Official sources first. Every programme and profile links to where the information came from, with the date we last checked it.
  • Free to use. No sign-up, no fees.
  • Kept up to date. Institutions change names, programmes and websites often. If you spot something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

What's next
